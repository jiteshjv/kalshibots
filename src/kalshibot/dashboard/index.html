Drawdown = the biggest drop from a running high to a later low, market by market. Capital in a market = the most money tied up in that market's open entries at once; only one market runs at a time, so the largest of these is the most ever tied up. Sharpe = average day ÷ day-to-day swing × √365. Calmar = yearly profit at the average day ÷ max drawdown. With only a few days of data these ratios mean little.
